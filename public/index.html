Why Students Switch from Standard Canvas to Slated

Factual comparison of day-to-day workflow differences for middle, high school, and university students.

Active Daily Gameplan vs. Cluttered To-Do List

Standard Canvas leaves months-old paper worksheets, in-class posterboards, and completed quarters as permanent overdue alarms. Slated automatically recognizes in-class and paper submissions, moves old units to archive, and prioritizes actionable work due within 48 hours.

Exact What-If Grade Simulation Weighted Math

Standard Canvas only supports raw points without factoring in category weights, dropped low scores, or locked assignments. Slated runs full category-weighted formulas to answer: "What exact percentage do I need on the final exam to maintain an A?"

Teacher Briefing & Key Extraction Direct Access

Teachers frequently bury quiz dates, homework problem numbers, and answer key links inside lengthy paragraphs. Slated extracts dates, files, and key bullet points into a clean, searchable updates feed.

School Bell Schedule & Calendar Feeds A/B Day Aligned

Canvas calendar feeds ignore high school period bells and lunch waves. Slated maps deadlines to your exact class period times and generates clean .ics calendar feeds for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook.

How to generate your Canvas access token

  1. Open Canvas → Account → Settings
  2. Scroll to Approved Integrations and click + New Access Token
  3. Name it Slated, set Expires to 30 days out, copy the token, and paste it on the right

Canvas tokens last 30 days. Slated reminds you 5 days before expiry and erases expired tokens from this device automatically. Tokens can be renewed anytime directly from your Canvas account settings.
